System Capabilities

Comprehensive Soil Health Platform

Integrated tools designed for scientific accuracy, actionable recommendations, and long-term land restoration.

Rule-Based Degradation Engine

Computes a deterministic 0–100 Soil Degradation Index evaluating pH balance, organic carbon, soil moisture, electrical conductivity, salinity, and erosion levels.

Interactive GIS Soil Mapping

Visualize geographic distribution of soil samples with color-coded degradation status pins using Leaflet.js and OpenStreetMap integration.

Tailored Conservation Advice

Automated generation of targeted sustainable practices including contour bunding, farmyard manure enrichment, gypsum treatment, and drip irrigation.

Dynamic Data Visualizations

Real-time analytical dashboards featuring Chart.js doughnut, bar, line, and radar charts tracking nutrient trends over time.

Multi-Sample Soil Comparison

Compare multiple soil samples side-by-side across pH, organic carbon, moisture, N-P-K, and salinity with clear comparative metrics.

PDF Report Generation

Export detailed, publication-ready PDF soil diagnostic reports and access full MySQL Workbench database documentation.
